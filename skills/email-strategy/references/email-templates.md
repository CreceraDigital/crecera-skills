## Flash Sale Templates

### Template 1: Flash Sale - Announcement ("Clock Starts Now")

**Subject patterns:**
- [48-Hour] Flash Sale starts now
- Today only: [X]% off [Product]
- Ends [DAY]: [Outcome] for [Price]

**Preheader:** Limited time - Ends [date/time] - See your picks inside

**Best used:** Sale opens; 24-72h window (BFCM, holiday, monthly promotion)

**Body structure:** Hook (what's on sale) → Value stack (who it helps) → Best-sellers/use-cases grid → Deadline box (exact timestamp, TZ) → FAQ (shipping/terms) → CTA

**Pro tips:**
- Put deadline + timezone above the fold
- Segment: exclude recent purchasers; add "VIP early access" variant
- Include plain-text mirror for inbox diversity

---

### Template 2: Flash Sale - Picks & Benefits ("Help Me Choose")

**Subject patterns:**
- Our picks under [€X] (sale on)
- Best for [use case]: save today

**Preheader:** Shortlist of winners + quick reasons

**Best used:** 12-24h after announcement

**Body structure:** Intro → Curated tiles (product → "best for" → quick proof) → FAQ → CTA

**Pro tips:** Pull dynamic picks from browse/cart history where possible

---

### Template 3: Flash/Launch - "Last Day AM"

**Subject patterns:**
- Ends tonight: [Outcome] for [Segment]
- Final day to get [bonus/price]

**Preheader:** Closes [time] [TZ] - details inside

**Best used:** Morning of final day (or 8-12h before close)

**Body structure:** Quick recap (benefit + offer) → Objection bullets → Social proof → Deadline module → CTA

**Pro tips:** Use shorter copy, bigger CTA, restate time left. Test "calendar hold" ICS.

---

### Template 4: Flash/Launch - "Final Hours" (plain-text)

**Subject patterns:**
- ⏳ Final hours: [Offer]
- Last chance before [time]

**Preheader:** Quick note from [Founder ] - closes soon

**Best used:** 1-4 hours before close

**Body structure:** 3 lines: reminder → outcome → single link

**Pro tips:** Send to non-clickers in last 72h; throttle if already clicked/purchased

## Extension

### Template 5: Extension (only if legit)

**Subject patterns:**
- Extended: [Offer] until [new time]
- By request: one more day

**Preheader:** New deadline: [date/time] - details

**Best used:** Real issues (payment gateway/traffic spike), small extension

**Body structure:** Reason → New deadline → What's included → CTA

**Pro tips:** State "no further extensions." Tag buyers of extension for future promos.

## Product Launch Templates

### Template 6: Product Launch - Teaser ("Why care?")

**Subject patterns:**
- Coming [DATE]: [Feature] → [Outcome]
- Sneak peek: solve [pain] in [time]

**Preheader:** Join the waitlist for bonus access

**Best used:** 3-14 days pre-launch

**Body structure:** Problem → What's changing (teaser) → Waitlist/early-bird CTA

**Pro tips:** Use one GIF to show the "aha"

---

### Template 7: Product Launch - Day 1 Offer

**Subject patterns:**
- It's live: [Product] is here (+ bonus)
- Unlock [Outcome] today

**Preheader:** Full tour, pricing, bonuses inside

**Best used:** Cart open

**Body structure:** Hook → Demo (gif/video) → Offer stack (what/price/bonus) → Risk reversal → CTA → P.S. deadline

**Pro tips:** Add comparison table vs. status quo

---

### Template 8: Launch - Case Study / Use-Case Deep Dive

**Subject patterns:**
- [Name] cut [metric] by [X]% with [Product]
- From [before] to [after] in [time]

**Preheader:** Exact steps + proof screenshots

**Best used:** Mid-launch

**Body structure:** Before → After → Steps → Screens → CTA

**Pro tips:** Lead with outcome, not features

---

### Template 9: FAQ / Objection Handler

**Subject patterns:**
- Is [Product] right for you? Short answers
- Answers to pricing, time, fit & more

**Preheader:** 5 quick bullets, 1 decision

**Best used:** Mid-launch; 48-24h pre-close

**Body structure:** Top 5 objections → One mini case → CTA

**Pro tips:** Use toggle/accordion style in HTML for scannability; provide plain-text fallback

## Comparison & Positioning

### Template 10: Comparison - "Us vs. Them"

**Subject patterns:**
- [Product] vs [Competitor]: what's best for [Segment]?
- Stop [pain ] - see the side-by-side

**Preheader:** Quick table + who should pick what

**Best used:** Considering alternatives; mid-launch; upgrade pushes

**Body structure:** "If you are… choose X" table → 3 proof points → CTA

**Pro tips:** Avoid mud-slinging; emphasize outcomes not features

---

### Template 11: Founder Letter ("From my desk")

**Subject patterns:**
- A personal note about [problem/outcome]
- Why we built [Product]

**Preheader:** Story + what it means for you

**Best used:** Launch week; price increase; apology; pivots

**Body structure:** Story (inciting incident) → Realization → Solution → Invitation (CTA)

**Pro tips:** Send plain-text with real signature; keep it under 300-400 words

## Feature & Price Updates

### Template 12: Feature Release ("What's new & why it matters")

**Subject patterns:**
- New: [Feature] → [Outcome] in [time]
- Now live: [Feature] (see 2 use cases)

**Preheader:** What changed - who benefits - how to try

**Best used:** Immediately after ship + within weekly digest

**Body structure:** Problem → What's new → Use cases → Docs link → CTA

**Pro tips:** Gate advanced features with upgrade CTA

---

### Template 13: Price Increase - Advance Notice / Lock-In

**Subject patterns:**
- Advance notice: pricing updates on [DATE]
- Keep your current price. Here's how

**Preheader:** What's changing - who's affected - options

**Best used:** 7-30 days before change

**Body structure:** Why change → What/when/who → Options (lock-in) → CTA → Help

**Pro tips:** Include grandfathering for goodwill; send reminder + last-day

## Trial & Lifecycle

### Template 14: Trial Ending Soon (SaaS)

**Subject patterns:**
- Your trial ends [DAY]: keep [Outcome] going
- Don't lose [value]: pick a plan

**Preheader:** Progress snapshot + next steps

**Best used:** T-3 days, T-1 day, T-0

**Body structure:** Usage stats → What you'll lose → Plan chooser → CTA

**Pro tips:** Add "book a 10-min call" secondary CTA for high-value users

---

### Template 15: Trial Ended - Win-Back

**Subject patterns:**
- Paused your [Outcome] - pick up where you left off
- We saved your [work/data] for [X] days

**Preheader:** 1-click reactivate + what's new

**Best used:** 1-7 days after expiry; again at 30 days with new value

**Body structure:** What's waiting → What's new → Offer/incentive → CTA

**Pro tips:** Offer temporary unlock of a premium feature to try again

---

### Template 16: Abandoned Cart / Checkout

**Subject patterns:**
- Still thinking about [Item]?
- Your [Item] is almost yours

**Preheader:** Answers to common questions inside

**Best used:** 1-4h, 24h, 48h after abandon

**Body structure:** Item visual → 3 benefits → Objection bullets → CTA

**Pro tips:** Avoid guilt copy ("forget something?"); consider ask-if-discount-helps on email #3

---

### Template 17: Activation Nudge ("Next best action")

**Subject patterns:**
- Do this next: [step] → [quick win]
- You're 1 step from [Outcome]

**Preheader:** Takes ~[time] - guided walkthrough

**Best used:** Day 1-7 of trial; triggered by inactivity

**Body structure:** Progress bar → Single task → Tiny tutorial (gif) → CTA

**Pro tips:** Personalize step based on what's incomplete

---

### Template 18: Re-engagement / Win-Back ("We miss you")

**Subject patterns:**
- Still want [Outcome]? Here's what's new
- Should we stop emailing you?

**Preheader:** Choose: keep tips, product updates, or pause

**Best used:** No click 60-90 days

**Body structure:** What's new → Value quick hits → Preferences (stay/pause/unsub) → CTA

**Pro tips:** One email focusing only on social proof can work well

## Post-Purchase

### Template 19: Post-Purchase Cross-Sell

**Subject patterns:**
- Because you bought [X] → you'll love [Y]
- Complete your setup: [Y] inside

**Preheader:** Pairs with [X] - quick add

**Best used:** Immediately after purchase or on delivery

**Body structure:** Order summary → Complement grid (why it pairs) → CTA

**Pro tips:** Reference the original item and usage timing

---

### Template 20: Replenishment / Usage Reminder

**Subject patterns:**
- Running low on [Product]?
- It's time to restock [X]

**Preheader:** Based on your last order date

**Best used:** Typical consumption cycle (e.g., 25-30 days)

**Body structure:** Days since purchase → Estimate remaining → 1-click reorder

**Pro tips:** Add subscription save-X% upsell

## Webinar Templates

### Template 21: Webinar - Invitation

**Subject patterns:**
- Live training: [Outcome] in [Time]
- Free workshop: [Bold promise] (+ Q&A)

**Preheader:** Spots limited - replay for registrants

**Best used:** 7-14 days before; resend to non-openers with new angle

**Body structure:** Who it's for → What you'll learn (3 bullets) → Hosts → Date/time → CTA

**Pro tips:** Auto-add calendar invite post-reg

---

### Template 22: Webinar - Reminders (24h & 1h)

**Subject patterns:**
- Starts [tomorrow/1 hour]: [Title]
- Your join link for [Title]

**Preheader:** Join link + worksheet attached

**Best used:** 24h and 1h pre-start

**Body structure:** Join link → Time → Bonus asset → CTA ("Add to calendar")

**Pro tips:** Include a text-only version for mobile

---

### Template 23: Webinar - Replay + Offer

**Subject patterns:**
- Replay: [Title] (+ limited bonus)
- Missed it? Watch by [DATE]

**Preheader:** Replay link - offer ends [time]

**Best used:** Immediately after; 48-72h window

**Body structure:** Replay link → Timestamps → Offer recap → Deadline → CTA

**Pro tips:** Add "skip to demo at 12:31" timestamps

## ROI & Social Proof

### Template 24: ROI / Calculator ("Do the math")

**Subject patterns:**
- What [Product] is worth to you (fast math)
- Save [€X]/mo by fixing [pain]

**Preheader:** Plug your numbers. See impact

**Best used:** Mid-funnel; before price increase

**Body structure:** Quick formula → Example → CTA to calculator → Case stat

**Pro tips:** Pre-fill fields if known; keep math above the fold

---

### Template 25: Social Proof Wall

**Subject patterns:**
- Why [N] teams choose [Product]
- Real results from people like you

**Preheader:** Logos + quotes + micro-metrics

**Best used:** Early-mid funnel; re-engagement

**Body structure:** Logo wall → Quote carousel → Mini-case tiles → CTA

**Pro tips:** Mix third-party ratings (G2/Capterra) with customer quotes

---

## Engagement & Bridge

### Template 26: Survey / NPS → Offer Bridge

**Subject patterns:**
- 2-minute check-in? (thank-you inside)
- Quick feedback → early access

**Preheader:** Help us improve, get [perk]

**Best used:** Post-purchase, 14-30 days; post-trial

**Body structure:** 1-question NPS → Optional comments → Perk reveal → CTA

**Pro tips:** Keep it frictionless (in-email buttons)

---

### Template 27: Content → Offer Bridge (lead magnet → paid)

**Subject patterns:**
- Liked [Guide]? Here's the next step
- Turn [tip] into [outcome] this week

**Preheader:** From reading to results: mini plan inside

**Best used:** 1-3 days after content download

**Body structure:** Reference content → 3-step mini plan → Offer match → CTA

**Pro tips:** Use UTM continuity from the magnet

---

### Template 28: "Short Personal Note" (Plain-text Nudge)

**Subject patterns:**
- Quick note re: [Product]
- [First name], is [outcome] still a priority?

**Preheader:** Saw you [action]; thought I'd share this

**Best used:** Final hours; sales conversations; high-intent segments

**Body structure:** Greeting → 2-3 lines → Single CTA → Signature

**Pro tips:** Reply-to founder; remove images/links except CTA

## Value & VIP

### Template 29: Price Justification / Value Recap

**Subject patterns:**
- What you get (and why it's priced this way)
- Where the value comes from

**Preheader:** ROI, support, roadmap: transparent breakdown

**Best used:** Mid-launch; before price increase

**Body structure:** Cost drivers → Outcomes → Proof → Comparison vs DIY/competitor → CTA

**Pro tips:** Include roadmap highlights to justify future value

---

### Template 30: VIP / Early Access (Segmented)

**Subject patterns:**
- [VIP Only] Early access + extra bonus
- You're on the list: get in early

**Preheader:** Limited slots - ends [DATE]

**Best used:** Tiered launch; top segments

**Body structure:** Why you're invited → Early access perk → Deadline → CTA

**Pro tips:** Require unique code to measure lift
