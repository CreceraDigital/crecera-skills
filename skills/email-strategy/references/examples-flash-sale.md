# Flash Sale / Discount Announcement Email Examples

These curated examples demonstrate effective techniques for flash sale and discount announcement emails. Each email has been selected for its psychological effectiveness, urgency mechanics, and conversion-focused copy.

---

## Example 1: The "Costly Mistake" Opener

**Why it works:** Opens with vulnerability and curiosity. Justifies the discount by framing it as a business risk, which increases perceived value. Short paragraphs maintain momentum.

**Subject:** Serious mistake

**Preheader:** 86% off iPhone Landscape Mastery for a very short time

**Body:**

I think I might be making a serious mistake...

Right now, I'm running a special sale...

And offering our iPhone Landscape Mastery course for an [incredibly low price][Link].

This is an exclusive limited-time offer just for you!

I know I couldn't pay my staff if the price was always this low...

But for a very short time, I wanted to make an exception.

I've seen the [incredible images][Link] Cliff's students take...

**Within hours, their landscape photos are transformed beyond their wildest dreams!**

But I know there are many people who can't afford the regular price...

So today I want to get our course into as many hands as I can:

[**Get 86% Discount**][Link]

## Example 2: The "Dollar Amount + Deadline" Combo

**Why it works:** Specific discount amount (4,000 off) makes savings tangible. "Tonight" deadline is clear and urgent. Ties to seasonal relevance (Black Friday prep). PS adds bonus value to sweeten the deal.

**Subject:** 4000 Off Ends Tonight

**Preheader:** Your Black Friday prep needs to start today...

**Body:**

Hi {First Name},

It's officially **Black Friday month**, and if your ads aren't ready, you're running out of time to [make it happen][Link].

Today is the **last day** to get **4,000 off** my **Mentorship Program**. This is your chance to get 1:1 guidance from me and my **$200 million team**, access daily live calls with us and more with our biggest discount yet.

With Black Friday just weeks away, now's the time to ensure your ads are fully optimised to **maximise your profits**.

Don't miss out. [**click here**][Link] to join my Mentorship Program before the offer expires tonight.

Talk soon,

Ben Heath

**P.S.** We also have an exclusive kit filled with invaluable tutorials that you can have for FREE when you join.

## Key Patterns Across These Examples

1. **Price justification**: "Couldn't pay my staff," "serious mistake," framing discount as exceptional
2. **Short paragraphs**: One idea per line, easy scanning, bold for emphasis
3. **Clear CTAs**: Action-oriented buttons that reinforce the offer
4. **Seasonal relevance**: Tying urgency to external events (Black Friday)
