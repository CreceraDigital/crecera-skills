# Final Hours / Last Chance Email Examples

These examples demonstrate genuine urgency without fake pressure. Use exact deadlines, real constraints, and simple copy.

---

## Example 1: The Direct Countdown

**Why it works:** Clear time constraint, simple structure, and one obvious CTA.

**Subject:** Ends tonight

**Preheader:** The launch price disappears at midnight

**Body:**

Quick reminder:

The launch price for [Product Name][Link] ends tonight at midnight Eastern.

After that, the price goes back to [Regular Price].

If you want the course, templates, and launch bonus at the current price, this is the last window to join.

[Join before midnight][Link]

Talk soon,

{Sender Name}

P.S. The deadline is midnight Eastern because this launch bonus is only available to the first cohort.

---

## Example 2: The Registration Close

**Why it works:** The urgency is about access, not just price. This works well for cohorts, workshops, or live programs.

**Subject:** Registration closes tonight

**Preheader:** Last call for this cohort

**Body:**

Enrollment for [Program Name][Link] closes tonight.

This cohort starts on [Start Date], and I need time to review everyone joining before we begin.

If you want the live sessions, implementation templates, and private support, join before registration closes at 11:59pm Pacific.

After that, the next chance to join will be [Next Available Window], if known.

[Save your spot][Link]

{Sender Name}

## Example 3: The Bonus Deadline

**Why it works:** Separates the main offer from the bonus deadline, which makes the reason for urgency easier to believe.

**Subject:** Bonus expires in 3 hours

**Preheader:** Join before 3pm to get the implementation review

**Body:**

You can still join [Product Name][Link] after today.

But the implementation review bonus expires at 3pm Eastern.

That bonus matters because it gives you a second set of eyes on your first draft. Instead of wondering whether you did it right, you get clear feedback and a next step.

If you want the review included, join before 3pm.

[Get the bonus][Link]

{Sender Name}

## Example 4: The Short B2B Deadline

**Why it works:** Busy readers get the context, deadline, benefit, and CTA in under a minute.

**Subject:** Last day for the early-bird rate

**Preheader:** Lock in the lower price before midnight

**Body:**

Hi {First Name},

Today is the last day to get the early-bird rate for [Program Name][Link].

If your team needs a cleaner way to [desired outcome], this gives you the process, templates, and support to get it implemented without guessing.

The price changes tonight at 11:59pm Eastern.

[Lock in the early-bird rate][Link]

{Sender Name}

P.S. If you have questions before joining, reply to this email and I will point you in the right direction.

---

## Key Patterns Across These Examples

1. **State the deadline early:** Do not bury the most important fact.
2. **Explain why the deadline exists:** Real constraints convert better than fake urgency.
3. **Keep final-hours copy short:** The reader is deciding, not researching.
4. **Use one CTA:** Make the action obvious.
5. **Avoid invented scarcity:** If there is no seat limit, use a price or bonus deadline instead.
