# Tips & Tricks for Email Strategy

Tactical implementation guidelines for high-converting email campaigns.

---

## Inbox Entry (From, Subject, Preheader)

- **From-name > everything.** Use a consistent human "From" that matches the relationship (founder for closers; brand for announcements). Changing it is a real test variable.
- **Subject lines: clarity + specificity + outcome.** Numbers, time bounds, and "action required" phrasing lift intent when honest. Avoid generic "Learn more." Keep it short-ish. Always A/B test.
- **Preheader = second subject.** Write it deliberately to extend/clarify the subject (50-100 chars). Hide visible preheaders unless they add value. Use the preview-text hack to control what shows.
- **Deadline visibility in the inbox.** Put a date/time + TZ in subject or preheader for sales and launches; pair with a plain-text "final hours" closer.
- **Don't chase opens.** Apple MPP inflates opens; optimize for clicks/conversions and base resends on non-clickers, not "non-openers."

---

## Copy & Structure That Converts (fast-scan friendly)

- **Design for scanners.** Lead with outcome, front-load key info, use subheads, bullets, short paragraphs; people read in an F-pattern.
- **One primary CTA.** Make it specific, benefit-led, and action verb first ("Get the training", "Start 14-day trial"). Avoid "Learn more." Support with a weaker secondary CTA only if necessary.
- **Use story & proof blocks.** Before/after mini-case + quantified metric beats feature lists for mid-launch emails.
- **Plain-text punches above its weight.** Especially for final-hours or founder notes; feels personal and cuts through clutter.
- **Exploit the P.S.** Summarize the offer/deadline or add one last proof/bonus; skimmers often read it. Use sparingly.

---

## Cadence & Pacing (what to send, when)

### Flash/Holiday Sale (48-72h)
Announce → Picks/benefits (T+12-24h) → Last day AM → Final hours (plain-text). Include exact close time/TZ; countdowns work.

### Launch (7-10 emails over 7-14 days)
Teaser → Problem agitation → Launch day offer/demo → Case/FAQ/Comparison → Last-day AM → Final hours. Keep closers short.

### Webinars
Invite (7-14d out) → Reminders (24h & 1h) → Replay + time-boxed offer.

### Resend Logic
If resending, change subject and/or From; for open-inflated audiences, target opened-no-click with adjusted CTA/offer instead of "non-openers."

---

## Segmentation & Personalization (high-impact, low-lift)

- **Behavior > demographics.** Segment by stage (lead/trial/active/churned), engagement (clicked last 30/60/90), and events (activated X, viewed pricing). Trigger on link clicks for fast interest buckets.
- **RFM (Recency-Frequency-Monetary).** Create VIP early access and at-risk segments; adjust offer/urgency accordingly.
- **Timezone-true deadlines.** Send last-day AM and final-hours per recipient's TZ; don't "spray" one global timestamp.

---

## Engagement Boosters (ethical persuasion)

- **Scarcity & urgency: show receipts.** Real end times, limited bonus stacks, or limited seats; pair with countdown visuals for clarity.
- **Comparison tables & ROI math** accelerate decisions mid-launch. Keep them outcome-first.
- **Proof walls.** Logos, quotes, micro-metrics sprinkled across the sequence outperform a single testimonial dump.
- **Interactive intent signals.** "Choose your use-case" links that segment on click (then follow up accordingly).

---

## Testing (fast, meaningful, MPP-aware)

- **Test what moves money.** Subject + preheader for click-through, not opens; CTA copy; angle (proof vs. ROI); From-name. Avoid micro-tests that won't scale.
- **Holdouts & sanity checks.** Keep a small no-send control on big promos to gauge incremental lift (then roll winning patterns into defaults).

---

## Deliverability & Compliance (copy-level)

- **Avoid deceptive patterns.** No fake "Re:" / "Fwd:" or misleading urgency. Keep punctuation/caps sane.
- **Words don't doom you: patterns do.** "Spam words" lists are overstated; reputation, engagement, and links matter more. Still, don't stuff sensational claims in subject lines.
- **Sunset unengaged.** Your re-engagement should offer a value reset or preferences; then suppress non-clickers to protect sender reputation.

---

## Angle & Copy Cheats (plug-and-play)

- **Outcome first:** "[Outcome] in [time] without [pain]" (subject/preheader + hero)
- **Objection bullets:** "No [time/skills/tool lock-in]" mid-email; link to FAQ/terms
- **CTA microcopy upgrades:** Verb + object + benefit ("Start the 7-day build") beats generic. Add immediacy words when true ("today," "now")
- **Founder note:** Plain-text story → invitation; great as a closer or price-update
- **P.S.:** Restate deadline/bonus or add tiny proof; keep to one line

---

## Cadence Blueprints

### BFCM/Holiday
Announce (specific savings + ends [date/TZ]) → Picks (use-case curation) → Last day AM → Final hours. Use timers only on truth-based deadlines.

### SaaS Launch
Teaser → Launch day demo/offer → Case/FAQ/Compare → Last day AM → Final hours (founder plain-text).

### Webinar
Invite (with 3 learning bullets) → 24h reminder → 1h reminder → Replay + time-boxed offer.

---

## TL;DR Defaults

- **Inbox:** clear subject + purposeful preheader; consistent human From
- **Structure:** scanner-friendly blocks; 1 primary CTA with outcome-based copy
- **Cadence:** announce → mid-proof/FAQ/compare → last-day AM → final hours (plain-text)
- **Segments:** stage + engagement + RFM; trigger on link-clicks; TZ-true deadlines
- **Measure:** clicks, activations, purchases: not opens (MPP)
