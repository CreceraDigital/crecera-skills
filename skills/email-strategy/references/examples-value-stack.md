# Value-Stack Email Examples

These examples show how to present bonuses, bundles, and offer components without turning the email into a wall of hype. Use them for structure and strategy, not for claims to copy.

---

## Example 1: Course Launch Bonus Stack

**What makes this effective:** The offer is broken into named components, each with a clear job. The copy explains why each bonus helps the buyer act faster.

**Subject:** Your launch bonuses expire tonight

**Preheader:** Templates, live help, and the fast-start checklist disappear at midnight

**Body:**

Enrollment for [Course Name][Link] closes tonight.

If you join before midnight, you also get the full launch bonus kit:

1. **Fast-Start Checklist**
   - Shows you exactly what to do in your first week
   - Removes the "where do I start?" problem
   - Helps you get a visible quick win before motivation fades

2. **Template Library**
   - Includes the worksheets, scripts, and planning docs used in the course
   - Saves you from building everything from scratch
   - Gives you a clean structure to customize for your business

3. **Live Implementation Session**
   - Bring your draft and get feedback
   - See how other students solve the same problems
   - Leave with the next version ready to use

The course gives you the system. The bonuses help you put it to work faster.

They disappear tonight because the live session and review window are only available to this launch group.

[Join before midnight][Link]

Talk soon,

{Sender Name}

P.S. If you already know you want the course, join now so you do not miss the implementation bonuses.

## Example 2: Service Package Value Recap

**What makes this effective:** Recaps the offer in plain language and ties each component to a business outcome. Good for a final-day email when readers need a quick decision aid.

**Subject:** Quick recap before the bonus expires

**Preheader:** Here is everything included when you join today

**Body:**

Quick recap before the early-bird bonus expires tonight.

When you join [Program Name][Link], you get:

- **Strategy audit:** We identify what is blocking growth and what to fix first.
- **Implementation roadmap:** You get a 30-day plan instead of a pile of random ideas.
- **Template pack:** You can adapt the exact pages, emails, and checklists instead of starting cold.
- **Review call:** We look at your first version and help you tighten the weak spots.
- **Private support:** You can ask questions when you get stuck.

The goal is simple: get you from "I should do this someday" to a working version you can actually use.

The review call bonus is only available until midnight because there are limited calendar slots.

[Claim the early-bird bonus][Link]

{Sender Name}

## Example 3: Product Bundle With Specific Use Cases

**What makes this effective:** Instead of listing a pile of products, it groups the bundle by use case. This makes the value easier to understand.

**Subject:** What is included in the bundle

**Preheader:** A quick breakdown by what each piece helps you do

**Body:**

The [Bundle Name][Link] is built around three jobs.

**Plan faster**

- Campaign planning board
- Weekly content planner
- Offer messaging worksheet

**Write better**

- Subject line bank
- Sales page outline
- Email sequence templates

**Launch cleaner**

- Deadline checklist
- Tracking sheet
- Post-launch review doc

You can use the pieces separately, but they work best together: plan the campaign, write the assets, launch, then review what worked.

The bundle discount ends Friday at 11:59pm Pacific.

[Get the bundle][Link]

{Sender Name}

## Key Patterns Across These Examples

1. **Name each component:** Make every bonus or bundle item easy to remember.
2. **Explain the job:** Tie each item to a practical outcome.
3. **Group related assets:** Use categories when the offer has many pieces.
4. **Use real scarcity:** Deadlines and limits need a believable reason.
5. **Keep the CTA simple:** One clear next step beats five competing links.
