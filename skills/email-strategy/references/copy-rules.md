## 1) Voice & Tone

- Write to one reader in second person ("you"), use active voice, short sentences, plain words. Front-load the point.
- Pick a tone on NN/g's 4 dimensions (humor, formality, respectfulness, enthusiasm) and keep it consistent. Casual-conversational often tests best for trust.
- Cut weasel words ("innovative," "world-class," "robust," "leading," "key") and vague qualifiers; replace with specific facts.

**Micro-swipes:**
- You-first rewrite: "We built X to…" → "You'll **{achieve outcome}** with X in **{timeframe}**."
- Specificity over superlatives: "industry-leading speed" → "**loads in 0.9s** on 4G."

## 2) Subject + Preview

- **Clarity > cleverness.** Short, descriptive subjects with a reason or outcome win more reliably than vague clickbait.
- Write subject + preview to read as one sentence.
- **Preview (40-130 chars)** should complete the subject (don't repeat it). Treat it as a second subject.
- Front-load key words; people scan the first characters.

**Subject swipes:**
- "**{Outcome}** without **{pain}**"
- "**New:** {feature} → **{benefit in time}**"
- "**Ends {day/time}:** {offer} for {segment}"
- "**Question:** Are you still {goal} by {date}?"

**Preview swipes:**
- "See how in **{mins}** • **{proof/bonus}** inside"
- "Closes **{time TZ}** • answers to pricing, fit, timing"

---

## 3) Hooks & Openings

- Say what/why/when in 2-3 lines: what's happening, why it matters, what to do. Users scan, not read; your first two lines carry the load.
- Use concrete problem → outcome language; avoid throat-clearing.

**Hook swipes:**
- "Quick heads-up: **{thing} is live**. Get **{outcome} in {time}**."
- "If **{pain}** costs you **{cost/time}**, this fixes it in **{time}**."

## 4) Body Structure (max impact order)

1. **Context** (pain/moment)
2. **Value/outcome** (benefits hierarchy)
3. **Proof** (metric, mini-story, named logo)
4. **How it works** (3 steps)
5. **Offer details** (price, terms, deadline)
6. **One primary CTA** (specific, verb-led)
7. **Objections/FAQ** (1-3 bullets)
8. **P.S.** recap benefit or alternate path (demo/reply)

**Proof swipes:**
- "**{N} teams** like **{logos}** cut **{metric}%** with **{feature}**."
- "From **{before}** to **{after}** in **{time}**. See screenshots."

## 5) CTA Microcopy

- Make the CTA answer "what do I get?"; avoid generic "learn more / click here."
- Prefer first- or second-person and benefit-led phrases ("Start **my** free trial," "Get **your** report").
- Add risk-reducers near the CTA ("no card," "cancel anytime") to remove friction.

**CTA swipes:**
- Primary: "**Start my 14-day trial →**", "**Get the ROI calculator →**"
- Safety net: "**Watch the 2-min demo →**"
- Reply path: "Questions? **Reply '?'** and I'll help."
- Frictionless: "**Download the {guide} (free) →**"
- Proof-CTA: "**See how {peer} did it →**"

## 6) Persuasion (ethical & effective)

- **Give a reason**: requests followed by "because {reason}" can increase compliance for low-effort asks; use real reasons.
- **Scarcity/urgency** works when genuine (limited time/slots). Overuse or fake constraints erodes trust.
- **Loss aversion framing** ("what you'll miss/lose") can lift action when used sparingly; don't make it manipulative.

**Urgency swipes (truth-based):**
- "Closes **{day, time, TZ}** **because** {real constraint}."
- "**{N} seats left** for live Q&A. We cap it so everyone gets answers."

---

## 7) Objection Handling

- **Price:** "Costs **{€}**, typically saves **{€ or time}** monthly. See the math."
- **Time:** "Setup takes **{mins}**; we import **{data}** for you."
- **Fit:** "Best for **{segment/use-case}**; not ideal if **{edge case}**."
- **Risk:** "**Try free {days}. No card. Cancel anytime.**"

Handle the top 3 objections in bullets; keep answers concrete.

---

## 8) Personalization & VoC

- Mirror voice of customer phrases (from reviews, support, forums) to increase relevance and credibility.
- Light personalization that changes the message (use-case, job-to-be-done) beats token {first_name} alone.

**VoC swipes:**
- "You told us **{quote fragment}**. Here's how **{feature}** solves it."
- "For **{role}** who **{task}**, this does **{outcome}** in **{time}**."

---

## 9) Pre-Built Line Bank

- **Problem → outcome:** "Struggling with **{pain}**? Here's how to **{outcome}** in **{time}**."
- **Value bullets (x3):** "Save **{N} hrs/week** on **{task}** • Cut **{cost/steps}%** • Hit **{goal}** without **{hassle}**."
- **Risk reversal:** "Try it **free {days}**. **No card**."
- **Deadline:** "Offer ends **{day/time/TZ}** **because** {reason}."
- **P.S.:** "Prefer to see it live? **Book a 10-min demo →**"

## 10) Style Guardrails

- **Plain language:** everyday words; one idea per paragraph; short sentences. (Aim ≈ Grade 6-8 unless writing to experts.)
- **No emdashes (—).** Never use the emdash character in any email copy, subject lines, or preheaders. Use a comma, period, colon, semicolon, or rewrite the sentence instead. This is a strict rule with zero exceptions. Emdashes are a well-known AI writing tell that erodes reader trust.
- **Banality filter:** replace "innovative, robust, world-class" with data (numbers, names, dates, time saved).
- **Ban filler CTAs:** no "learn more/click here"; write what happens next.
- **Front-load meaning** in subjects, headers, bullets; readers scan the left and top.

## 11) Compact Patterns (drop-in skeletons)

### A) Launch/Sale Email (short)

- **Subject:** "{Outcome} today: {offer} ends {time}"
- **Preview:** "See {proof} + bonus inside."
- **Open:** "It's live: {what} → {benefit}."
- Benefit bullets (x3)
- Proof line
- **CTA:** "Get {offer} →" (subtext: "no card / cancel anytime" if relevant)
- FAQ bullets (price/fit/time)
- P.S. alternate path

### B) FAQ/Objection

- **Subject:** "Is {product} right for you? short answers."
- **Open:** "Top questions in 30 seconds:"
- Bullets: price • fit • time • risk (one-liners)
- Mini-case + CTA

### C) Founder Plain-Text Closer

- **Subject:** "final hours: {offer}"
- **Open:** 2 lines: reminder + because-reason + deadline
- Single link
- Signature

## 12) Copy Tests Worth Running

- **Subject:** clarity vs curiosity; number/time specific vs not; with/without urgency
- **Preview:** benefit vs proof; count of characters
- **CTA:** first-person vs second-person; benefit-led vs generic
- **Angle:** proof-led vs ROI-math vs story

---

## 13) Editing Checklist

- Does line 1-3 state what/why/when + 1 action? (if not, rewrite)
- Are we using "you," active verbs, plain words? (replace passives/jargon)
- Swap vague claims for specifics (numbers, names, time, dates)
- CTA says the outcome (ban "learn/click more")
- One primary CTA; objections answered in 1-3 bullets
- Ethical urgency only, with a reason ("because …")
- Zero emdashes (—) anywhere in the copy (replace with comma, period, colon, or rewrite)
